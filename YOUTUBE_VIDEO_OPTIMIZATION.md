## 16. TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

**If Views Are Low:**
- Improve thumbnail (test 3 variations)
- Change title to be more clickable
- Share more widely on social media
- Join and post in niche Facebook groups
- Consider YouTube ads ($50 test)

**If Watch Time Is Low (Under 40%):**
- Hook isn't strong enough (re-edit first 10 seconds)
- Video too long (cut to under 3 minutes)
- Pacing too slow (add jump cuts)
- Add pattern interrupts every 15-20 seconds

**If CTR to Demo Is Low:**
- Make CTA more prominent in video
- Add verbal CTA every 30 seconds
- Improve pinned comment
- Test different CTAs (urgency, social proof, etc.)
- Add on-screen graphics with link

**If Demo Show-Up Rate Is Low:**
- Send confirmation email immediately
- Send reminder 24 hours before
- Send reminder 1 hour before
- Make booking process easier
- Offer calendar integration
